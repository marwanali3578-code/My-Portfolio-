03 — FEATURED PROJECT

Auto-Reply Bot

Turning "the same five questions" into an instant reply
Python Keyword-Matching Logic Training Project
THE PROBLEM
Small business owners — shop owners, service providers, freelancers — get the same handful of questions from customers every day: prices, working hours, availability, location. Answering each one by hand eats into hours that could go toward actual work, and a slow reply can cost a customer who moves on to a competitor.
THE APPROACH
I built a Python bot that reads incoming messages, matches them against a business's most common questions using keyword detection, and replies instantly with accurate, pre-approved answers. Anything it doesn't recognize is flagged for the owner to answer personally — so every customer still gets a real answer, just faster.
MANUAL VS. AUTOMATED
BEFORE — MANUAL, PER CUSTOMER
Customer sends a question
Owner reads it, mid-task
Owner searches for the answer
Owner types out a reply
Repeat for every customer, every day
AFTER — AUTOMATED
Customer sends a question
Bot replies instantly, 24/7
Owner only steps in for the unusual ones
STATUS
Built and tested as a training project on the DEPI Automation track. Ready to be adapted to a real business's own FAQs, tone, and platform — WhatsApp, Instagram, or email.
